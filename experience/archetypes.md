# Scene archetypes (rapid read → country cluster)

Seed from the geo-sleuth skill's `references/rapid-triage.md`; **extend this
file as new photos get solved.** Each entry narrows to a *cluster*, not a single
country — the refine stage confirms the member.

Format:

```
### <name>
- Look for: ...
- Points to (cluster, first → runners-up): ...
- Strength: rapid-only | strong | medium | weak
- Separators / counterexamples: ...
- Source: <date / which run>
```

---

### Dike / causeway road across water on both sides
- Look for: straight road riding an embankment, shallow **reedy** water on *both*
  sides, green narrow verge, very big sky, lattice pylons.
- Points to: **Netherlands** (Markermeer/IJsselmeer, Houtribdijk N302,
  Flevoland dikes) → Denmark (Limfjord / Agger & Harboøre Tange), Germany (North
  Frisia), Poland (Vistula Spit), Lithuania (Curonian Spit).
- Strength: rapid-only (NW-European dike country), weak between members.
- Separators: reeds + fresh/brackish shallows = Dutch polder/dike; salt marsh +
  one sea side = North-Sea coast; sign language and guardrail post shape split
  NL from DK/DE.
- Source: 2026-10-13 tuxun global #3 (rapid-only, unverified).

### Post-Soviet streetscape (under a bridge / city edge)
- Look for: tall poles with a **curved arm + single rectangular luminaire**
  (often mixed vintages in one frame), **blue/white rectangular signs**,
  concrete abutment with **large graffiti**, cracked patch asphalt, a worn dirt
  path, many low-voltage wires.
- Points to: **Russia** (mid-size Volga/Kama city) → Kazakhstan, Belarus,
  Ukraine, Serbia/Bosnia.
- Strength: rapid-only (post-Soviet sphere), weak between members.
- Separators: sign text script (Cyrillic vs Latin); bin/pole details; **NOT
  China** — no Han characters, different lamp archetype.
- Source: 2026-10-13 tuxun global #2 (rapid-only, unverified).

### Mediterranean olive–limestone coast road
- Look for: silver-leaf **olive groves**, dry-stone terraces, **white W-beam
  guardrail with round posts**, a **green municipal bin**, the sea with a large
  landmass across a wide channel.
- Points to: **Greece** (Ionian: Corfu/Lefkada; or Peloponnese north shore) →
  Croatia (Dalmatia), Turkey (Aegean), Italy (Sicily/Sardinia).
- Strength: rapid-only, weak between members.
- Separators: green municipal bin + white round-post guardrail favors GR/IT;
  Croatian bins differ; Turkish fixtures run grey; Italian roads look "tidier";
  island vs mainland decides Ionian vs Peloponnese.
- Source: 2026-10-13 tuxun global #4 (rapid-only, unverified).

### Andean / Latin-American red-earth urban street (NEGATIVE-clue entry)
- Look for: red-earth tone, a cordillera silhouette in frame, Spanish-language
  **street signage**, mid-rise concrete/plaster buildings.
- ⚠️ **Trap:** this reads "plausible Spanish-speaking country" and invites a
  confabulated guess (Mexico / Chile / Peru / Ecuador are all fair). A single
  Spanish-sign + red-soil cue is **not** sufficient to name one country.
- Points to: a *region* only — **Andes / Spanish-America**, never collapse to
  one member on weak evidence.
- Strength: weak as a single-country cue; promote to a *decline* or *region* read
  unless ≥2 cross-confirming specifics appear (altiplano village form, coastal
  desert context, distinct sign typography).
- Source: 2026-10-14 blind R1 t06 — truth Peru, misread as Mexico/Chile (miss).

### Road-sign family (Vienna vs MUTCD) — sign-shaped cluster read
- Look for: the **warning-sign shape/border** first, then STOP wording and
  direction-board colour. Red upright triangle → Vienna/continental Europe
  family; yellow diamond → MUTCD family (US/CA/AU/JP) **or Ireland**.
- Points to: a *sign-standard cluster*, not a country — see `ROAD_SIGNS.md`.
  Pairs naturally with script + driving side as the second independent clue.
- Strength: rapid-only (cluster-level), medium with a second clue; never
  excludes on one sign.
- Separators / counterexamples: amber-filled red triangles in Nordic/Balkan
  snow countries; black-border yellow triangle in mainland China; white/red
  triangle in Taiwan (China); Ireland's yellow diamonds; Japan's diamond +
  止まれ inverted-triangle STOP; Germany's colour-by-road-class boards.
- Source: public sign manuals cross-checked 2026-11-12 (UNECE Vienna
  Convention, MUTCD, StVO, TII); **unverified — not yet confirmed on a solved
  photo in this project.** Promote after a real case.

### UK vs Ireland — the left-driving look-alike pair
- Look for: both are LHT and English-speaking, so driving side cannot split
  them. Read the **warning sign** (UK red triangle / IE yellow diamond), the
  **unit** (UK mph / IE km/h) and **bilingual Gaeilge** place names.
- Points to: Ireland when yellow diamond + bilingual + km/h agree → UK otherwise.
- Strength: rapid-only, weak between members unless ≥2 of the three agree.
- Separators / counterexamples: Northern Ireland (part of the UK) uses UK signs
  and mph; Gaeltacht signs can be Irish-only; motorway boards are blue in both.
- Source: `ROAD_SIGNS.md` §1a (RSA *Rules of the Road* / TII manual,
  cross-checked 2026-11-12); **unverified on a solved case**.
